# Argument Arena Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

Argument Arena processes Discord identifiers, debate messages and revisions, match settings,
results, training activity and optional profile insights to operate the bot. Debates and
results are visible according to server permissions. Profile analysis requires opt-in.

Enabled AI features send relevant content to OpenCode Go and its selected model provider.
Research uses search providers and public websites. Their retention policies apply. We do
not sell personal information or use Discord messages to train AI models.

Records and backups may remain until the operator removes them. Deleting a Discord message
or profile insight does not erase every stored copy. For access, correction, deletion or
other privacy questions, contact your server administrator to reach the bot operator, or
[request a private contact method](https://github.com/KianBahasadri/argument-arena/issues/new).
Do not post private information in public issues. Applicable privacy rights remain unchanged.
