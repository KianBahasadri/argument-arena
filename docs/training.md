# Training

`training.py` stores reasoning practice sessions and progress. `member_ui.py`
provides the private `/train` menu, mode/difficulty pickers and answer forms.
Sessions are scoped to a human member and server; each member can have one active
or grading session at a time.

## Exercises

Modes are `spot`, `selected`, `mixed`, `battle`, `repair` and `sound`. Selected
practice requires a supported fallacy. Difficulty is 1–3, and topics contain
1–100 characters. Repair asks for improved reasoning; sound practice alternates
valid and fallacious examples across completed attempts. A battle has three
graded turns; other modes have one.

`curriculum.py` contains ten original fallacy families, aliases, teaching notes,
repairs, hints and scenario variants. Reference links credit Bo Bennett's
Logically Fallacious; the code does not copy that site's examples. The bot's
artwork and its generation prompt live under `assets/`.

Family selection initially rotates toward the least-practiced category. After
coverage, recent performance can guide selection. Opted-in profile recommendations
can influence practice selection every third completed attempt. Difficulty adds
context and distractors to prepared scenarios. The public exercise omits hidden
answers; selected practice exposes its reference link.

## Answers and grading

Answers require an explanation of 10–4000 characters. Submitting persists the
answer, changes the session to `grading` and queues a content-keyed `train_grade`
job. Another answer cannot replace it while grading is pending. Hint requests
increment a saved count.

The LLM grader receives the original exercise, mode, difficulty and saved answer.
It must return identification and explanation scores in 0–1, feedback and an
optional alternative label. The final score is 30% identification and 70%
explanation. Results include teaching notes, repair, reference, hints and the
submitted answer. Duplicate delivery cannot append progress twice.

In a battle, later turns can be generated by the LLM with a grounded planted
fallacy quote. If generation fails, grading remains durable and the next turn
uses an original prepared curriculum exercise with an explicit fallback notice.
Provider policy is described in [AI services](ai-services.md).

## Progress and recovery

Progress tracks attempts by fallacy, overall/recent scores, explanation and
identification performance, and hint usage. Recommendations prioritize the three
weakest recent categories. Suggested difficulty is bounded to 1–3 and derives
from strong results among the last ten attempts.

**Current exercise** exposes grading status and safe recovery instructions.
**Retry failed grading** requeues failed jobs for the current owner's saved
session, preserving their answer and applying the same budgets. **Stop session**
retains completed practice. Training never changes competitive Elo. Sharing
training summaries through a profile is separately controlled as described in
[profiles and ratings](profiles-and-ratings.md).
